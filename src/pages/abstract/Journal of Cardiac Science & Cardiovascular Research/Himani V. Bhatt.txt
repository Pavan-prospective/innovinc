ERACS and Multimodal Analgesia: is this is the answer to the Opioid Crisis in Cardiac patients?

Himani V. Bhatt, DO, MPA, FASE, FASA

Mount Sinai Health systems, United States

Opioids are still considered mainstay for cardiac surgical patients. However, due to the need reduce opioid use and optimize recovery, there has been a significant push to implement multimodal techniques to reduce the opioid use in cardiac surgery. Many facets of multimodal analgesia have been incorporated into the goals of Enhanced Recovery Models/Clinical Pathways. Here we will review multiple components of multimodal analgesia for a successful ERACS model and reduce opioid use in the cardiac surgical patient population.

Objectives: 

Review the current condition on the opioid use in cardiac surgical patient and briefly review components of ERACS (Enhanced Recovery after Cardiac Surgery) and its utility in cardiac surgery

Discuss pharmacological management and review current literature

Review the application of regional techniques for cardiac and cardiac-related procedures
