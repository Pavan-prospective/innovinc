Journal of Nursing

About the Journal: The Journal of Nursing is an international, peer-reviewed scholarly journal dedicated to advancing nursing science, clinical practice, nursing education, healthcare leadership, and evidence-based patient care. The journal serves as a global platform for nurses, clinicians, educators, researchers, and healthcare professionals to publish high-quality original research, systematic reviews, clinical studies, case reports, and innovative healthcare practices that contribute to improving patient outcomes and strengthening nursing practice across diverse healthcare settings. 
The journal welcomes multidisciplinary research covering medical-surgical nursing, critical care, pediatric and neonatal nursing, mental health, oncology, community health, geriatric care, infection prevention, digital health, nursing informatics, healthcare quality improvement, and nursing leadership. By promoting scientific excellence and encouraging international collaboration, the journal aims to bridge the gap between research and clinical practice while supporting continuous professional development and innovation in modern nursing.

Latest News:
WHO Launches New Global Nursing and Midwifery Workforce Strategy (2026):- 
The World Health Organization and international health partners have introduced updated strategies to strengthen the global nursing and midwifery workforce. The initiative emphasizes workforce retention, leadership development, expanded education, digital competencies, and equitable access to nursing care, aiming to address the projected shortage of healthcare professionals over the coming decade.
Telehealth and Community Nursing Improve Patient Care Outcomes:- Healthcare systems are increasingly integrating telehealth, remote patient monitoring, and community-based nursing to improve access to healthcare services, particularly in rural and underserved regions. These digital care models enhance chronic disease management, rehabilitation, preventive care, and patient education while enabling nurses to deliver high-quality care beyond traditional hospital settings.


Nursing Education Advances Through Scholarships and Digital Learning:-
Healthcare organizations and academic institutions continue to invest in nursing education through scholarships, simulation-based learning, and advanced clinical training programs. Recent initiatives are helping nurses pursue higher education, strengthen evidence-based practice, and prepare for leadership roles in critical care, public health, and community nursing, ensuring a skilled future workforce.
AI Transforms Nursing Education and Clinical Decision-Making:- 
Artificial intelligence is rapidly reshaping nursing education and clinical practice. Universities and hospitals are adopting AI-powered simulation training, virtual patients, electronic clinical decision support, and predictive analytics to improve nursing competencies, reduce medical errors, and enhance patient safety. As digital healthcare continues to expand, nursing informatics and AI literacy are becoming essential skills for the future nursing workforce.

16. Palliative Care Explores symptom management, supportive care, and end-of-life nursing. Focuses on improving quality of life through compassionate, patient-centered, and multidisciplinary care.
17. Globalization and Health Examines global health challenges, international healthcare collaboration, and health equity. Covers emerging diseases, cross-border healthcare initiatives, and strategies to strengthen global health systems.
18. Health Care Systems Focuses on healthcare policy, service delivery models, and system-wide innovations. Highlights research that improves healthcare accessibility, quality, efficiency, and sustainability
